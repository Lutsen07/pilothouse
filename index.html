Words we use

Short forms, said in plain English.

SOP

Standard operating procedure. The written steps for one job, such as receiving a carton.

SKU

Stock keeping unit. The code for one sellable item, like the blue 12 ounce mug.

PO

Purchase order. The order you send a vendor when you buy stock.

3PL

Third party logistics. An outside warehouse that picks, packs, and ships for you.

RMA

Return merchandise authorization. The number you give a return so you can find it later.

QA

Quality assurance. A check that a listing or a job is right before it goes live.
