Words we use

Short forms, said in plain English.

SOP

Standard operating procedure. The written steps for one job, such as receiving a carton.

SKU

Stock keeping unit. The code for one sellable item, like the blue 12 ounce mug.

PO

Purchase order. The order you send a vendor when you buy stock.

3PL

Third party logistics. An outside warehouse that picks, packs, and ships for you.

RMA

Return merchandise authorization. The number you give a return so you can find it later.

QA

Quality assurance. A check that a listing or a job is right before it goes live.

Yes, if you run a small shop yourself. It is a weekly habit for the person who owns the place and still packs boxes. It is not mentoring, and it is not help for trucking owner-operators.

I am just starting. Is this too soon?

This is the right time, and for a new business start it is the critical time. Write receiving, packing, returns, and Monday before the first rush so the work does not live only in your head. The same fire every week. Late vendors, returns with no reason, listings that say in stock when the bin is empty. You get a Monday meeting that ends and written steps so the leak cannot hide.

No. Word, Excel or Numbers, and a calendar file. If you already have a warehouse system people actually use, you do not need this kit.
